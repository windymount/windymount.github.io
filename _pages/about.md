Hi! I'm a fourth year Ph.D. student in the Department of [Computer Science and Engineering](https://cse.ucsd.edu/) at UC San Diego, in the Trustworthy ML Lab. I'm fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Tsui-Wei (Lily) Weng](https://lilywenglab.github.io/). My research interest is in **trustworthy machine learning and responsible AI**, with a focus on **mechanistic interpretability**, **uncertainty quantification**, and **robust prediction**. Recently, I have been using representation engineering and model editing to understand and improve LLM reasoning, reflection, and tool use. My CV could be found [here](files/cv.pdf).
